## Q1: [Securities Crime] For Expatriates: How to protect trademark rights against infringement?

**A**: Trademark infringement remedies: 1) Collect and preserve evidence including notarized purchases; 2) Send cease and desist letter; 3) File complaint with market supervision authority; 4) File lawsuit for injunction and damages; 5) Compensation based on actual losses, infringer profits, or royalty multiples.

**法律依据**: 《商标法》

**领域**: 知识产权 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Real Estate Disputes] For Overseas Chinese: How is property divided in cross-border divorce?

**A**: Cross-border divorce property division involves multiple legal systems: 1) Determine jurisdiction court first; 2) Chinese property follows Chinese law, overseas property may follow local law; 3) Different countries have different standards for marital property; 4) Prenuptial agreements recommended.

**法律依据**: 《民事诉讼法》涉外编

**领域**: 婚姻家事 | **语言**: English
